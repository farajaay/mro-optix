01 / THE WORKFLOW

From a material request
to a recorded decision.

A clear route from demand to evidence to human approval.

01

Bring the evidence

Bring supplied request, master, consumption history, BOM, stock and open-PO data together. Resolve missing keys, unit mismatches and incomplete records first.

02

Make the decision explicit

Compare requested quantity with the available evidence. Keep the proposal separate from the engineer’s approved quantity, with an owner, date and reference.

03

Review supply against the need date

Distinguish usable stock, on-time PO supply, late supply and additional purchase need before directing an action.

Engineering retains the decision.

The confirmed basis stays with the decision. Changes to inputs require reconfirmation. The tool does not issue automatic approval.

What has been tested so far?

The candidate record reports 29 passed dynamic checks and zero formula-error matches in the final blank and demo scans. Importer testing reports 22 passes plus one recorded harness mismatch concerning preservation of the leading zero in LIMIT-0245. LibreOffice checks do not constitute native Windows Excel acceptance. Worksheet protection is not enabled or certified in this candidate.

Is the workbook available in Arabic?

The website is available in Arabic and English. Product workbooks and documentation are in English; the Arabic website does not imply an Arabic workbook.

Is this a web app or a connected ERP tool?

The product consists of Excel workbooks and an optional importer. It uses supplied source data and has no live connection to SAP, Maximo, Oracle or other ERP systems.

Does it guarantee savings or material availability?

No. A proposed quantity difference is not a realised saving or a purchase approval. Verify source data and outputs, and follow site controls before action.
