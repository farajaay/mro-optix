One review path from request to decision.

Designed for practitioners who already work from spreadsheet extracts and need a disciplined view of data exceptions, quantity evidence, human approval, and need-date supply.

01

Bring supplied data

Use request, master, material/plant history, BOM, stock, and open-PO files. Expose missing identifiers, unit mismatches, and incomplete records before quantity discussion.

02

Review evidence

Compare the request with supplied BOM and comparable-event history. Keep a model proposal separate from the quantity approved by the responsible engineering reviewer.

03

See need-date supply

Distinguish usable stock, on-time PO quantity, late PO quantity, and additional purchase need. Verify the outputs before procurement or maintenance action.

Know the fit before the offer.

This Individual edition is intentionally bounded. A large operation may still have a suitable focused review; the limits below are hard and must not be worked around by truncating data.

Potential fit

  • One hands-on turnaround or maintenance material reviewer
  • English working documents
  • Microsoft 365 Excel for Windows, after native acceptance
  • One organisation and one named industrial site
  • Supplied, verified source extracts and a responsible engineering reviewer

Hard limits

  • 250 request lines
  • 500 master-data records
  • 500 material/plant consumption-history records
  • 500 BOM records
  • Eight comparable turnaround-event slots per material/plant
  • One open-PO quantity and due date per material/plant
  • Imported source text limited to 250 characters per field

Not included

Live SAP, Maximo, Oracle, or other ERP integration; multiple dated receipt lines per material/plant; automatic engineering approval; an immutable multi-user audit trail; turnaround scheduling; stock transfers; expiry calculations; live kit status; multi-user, multi-site, or cross-client consulting rights.

Frequently asked questions.

Direct answers about availability, compatibility, data handling, and responsibility.

Can I buy MRO Optix Individual v2.1 now?

No. It is a candidate release, not approved for sale. Checkout remains disabled while native Microsoft 365 Excel for Windows acceptance, worksheet-protection validation, seller verification, and delivery checks are pending.

Does it connect to an ERP?

No. There is no live SAP, Maximo, Oracle, or other ERP integration. The workflow uses supplied files and does not write back to an ERP.

Does the workbook approve engineering changes?

No. It supports review and keeps proposed and approved quantities distinct. A responsible person must verify source data and confirm the engineering, procurement, or maintenance action.

Which Excel environment is targeted?

Microsoft 365 Excel for Windows is the target, but native acceptance of the exact archive is still pending. Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets, and LibreOffice are not certified target environments.

Is worksheet protection a security feature?

No. Protection is not certified or enabled in this candidate. Its intended purpose is to reduce accidental formula edits while allowing intended inputs; it is not access control, confidentiality, or data security.

Does the product upload operational data?

The product files operate locally and do not automatically send workbook data to the seller. This site does not request plant, maintenance, inventory, or engineering uploads. Use only anonymised examples in any future support interaction.

Does a proposed reduction represent savings?

No. A proposed reduction is a review output, not an approved quantity, realised saving, cash saving, or guaranteed outcome.

Can consultants use one licence across clients?

No. The planned Individual licence is for one named user, one organisation, and one named industrial site. Cross-client, multi-user, and multi-site use require separate written terms.
